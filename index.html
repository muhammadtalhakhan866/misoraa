Craftsmanship

How Every Watch Is Made.

01

The Calibre

Swiss-grade automatic movements, regulated in five positions and tested over 14 days before a case ever closes around them.

02

The Finish

18K gold PVD over surgical steel, applied in six passes — a finish measured in microns, not marketing claims.

03

The Seal

Every case is pressure-tested individually. Screw-down crowns, triple gaskets, and a caseback torqued to spec by hand.

04

The Promise

An 18-month international warranty, lifetime servicing at cost, and a certificate numbered to your piece alone.
