A cushion case is harder to finish than a round one: four flanks meet four corners, and every one of those transitions has to be polished by hand without losing the line. The flanks are brushed, the bevels mirror-polished, and the crown is set low into the right flank so it does not press on the wrist. Thirty metres is splash resistance — take it off before you swim.

Insured delivery is free worldwide and ships within 48 hours. Every piece travels in a sealed presentation case with its certificate. If it is not right, return it unworn within 30 days for a full refund — we cover the return shipping.

Eighteen months international warranty against any fault in the movement or finish. After that, servicing is offered at cost for the life of the watch — send it to the atelier and it comes back regulated, resealed and pressure-tested.
